In plain words

A robot lab hunting for two cancer drugs that work better together

Think of it as searching for the best two-ingredient recipe when every taste test takes two days. There are thousands of possible dose pairs, too many to try them all, so the lab tries a few, learns, and decides what to try next. Nobody steps in.

  1. ChoosePick a handful of dose pairs worth testing.
  2. MixThe pipetting robot fills a plate: cancer cells plus the two drugs in each tiny well.
  3. Wait and measureTwo days in the incubator, then a reader checks how many cells survived.
  4. LearnUpdate the guess of where the best pair is, and choose again.
↺ repeat until the best pair is found

Synergy is the "salt and caramel" effect: the pair does more than you would expect from each drug alone. It usually hides in a small patch of the dose map, which is why searching smartly beats testing everything.
