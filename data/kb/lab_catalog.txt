Section 1. Assay plates

All assay plates are 384-well, tissue-culture treated, sterile, with lids. Order by part number. Items not listed here are not stocked.

PL-384-CLR  Clear polystyrene walls and clear bottom. Stockroom default for cell culture, microscopy and absorbance assays. Light passes through the walls between wells.
PL-384-WHT  Solid white opaque walls and white bottom. Reflects light; for luminescence assays.
PL-384-BLK  Solid black opaque walls and black bottom. Absorbs light; for fluorescence assays with low background. Luminescence signal is reduced compared to white plates.

Working volume for all three plates: 10 to 100 ul per well.
Section 2. Liquid handler tips and pipetting

Tips for the Hamilton STARlet 8-channel head (CO-RE, filtered, sterile, 96 per rack): TIP-50 (50 ul), TIP-300 (300 ul), TIP-1000 (1000 ul). A volume larger than the tip holds is pipetted in several equal parts.

Calibrated volume ranges, from the Hamilton STAR liquid classes (as shipped with PyLabRobot 0.2.2). Outside its range a liquid class is not calibrated: expect less liquid than asked and more spread between channels. Culture medium and buffers pipette like water.
50 ul tip: medium jet 10 to 50 ul; medium surface 1 to 50 ul; dmso jet 10 to 50 ul; dmso surface 1 to 50 ul.
300 ul tip: medium jet 20 to 300 ul; medium surface 0.5 to 300 ul; dmso jet 20 to 300 ul; dmso surface 1 to 300 ul.
1000 ul tip: medium jet 10 to 1000 ul; medium surface 10 to 1000 ul; dmso jet 5 to 1000 ul; dmso surface 10 to 1000 ul.

Dispense modes. Jet: the drop leaves the tip above the well, so one aspiration can serve many wells of the same reagent. Surface: the tip touches the well bottom or the liquid already in it to release the drop. A reused tip that touches liquid in a well carries that well's contents to the next wells it serves; a tip that only touches a dry, empty well does not.

Current protocol volumes on 384-well plates: cell suspension 16 ul per well; each drug dilution 2 ul per well.
Section 3. Reservoirs and source plates

RES-CELL    60 ml single-well trough (Hamilton 1-trough 60 ml) for one cell suspension. All 8 channels draw from it. Use one reservoir per cell line; the cell prep station fills a reservoir to order (counted cells in culture medium) and the arm brings it to the liquid handler deck.
SRC-DIL     Drug dilution plate, 96 deep-well, 1.2 ml maximum per well. One column per dose of one drug (rows A to H feed channels 1 to 8). The liquid handler prepares dilution series in it on the deck.
TUBE-2ML    2 ml screw-cap tube for drug stock solutions.
